Ask a statistical question about 1.85M Goodreads editions.

A model chooses the tool and writes the explanation. It does not write the numbers: every figure below is rendered from the tool's own JSON, together with the count it rests on, the unit one row counts, what the minimum-ratings threshold excluded, and the caveats attached to the specific columns they weaken.

  • The tool call is shown in full — name, parameters, bytes billed, cache hit or miss, and the BigQuery share of the wall time.
  • A refused call is a result, not an error. Asking for something the dataset cannot support tells you why it cannot.
  • A checker marks any numeral in the prose that does not appear in a tool result, so the interface's central claim is verifiable rather than asserted.
